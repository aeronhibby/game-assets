Privacy Policy for Kelsi Davies: Haunt Escape

Hibby Games built 'Kelsi Davies: Haunt Escape' as a commercial app.

Data Collection & Third-Party Services
We do not directly collect, harvest, or store personal user data on private servers. However, this game integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to support gameplay analytics, ad mediation, and advertisement delivery. These third parties may collect data, including but not limited to device information, device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and gameplay performance metrics.

You can review the privacy policies of our integrated partners below:
Unity (Engine & Analytics): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
Unity LevelPlay (Mediation): https://unity.com/legal/developer-privacy-policy
Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
