# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026  
**Developer:** Hibby Games (Aaron Hibberd)  
**Covered Applications:** Kelsi Davies: Haunt Escape; Taya's Alphabet (Android and iOS)  
**Contact Email:** contact@hibbygames.com  

Hibby Games ("we," "our," or "us") develops and publishes mobile games, including **Kelsi Davies: Haunt Escape** and **Taya's Alphabet** (collectively, the "Games"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play our Games on Android or iOS, how it is used, and what choices you have.

## 1. Overview & Third-Party Black-Box Disclosure
We do not operate our own backend servers to store your personal information. Instead, our Games integrate trusted third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for game engine functionality, analytics, advertising, platform achievements, in-app purchases, and optional push notifications.

Because these third-party SDKs are maintained by independent companies (such as Unity and Google) and may be updated over time, those providers process data automatically according to their own technical implementations and privacy policies. Below is a comprehensive summary of the categories of data these services may collect across our Games.

## 2. Information Collected Automatically Through Third-Party Services
Depending on which Game you play, your device platform (Android or iOS), and the features you use, our integrated SDKs may automatically collect:

* **Device and Technical Information:** Device model, operating system and version, screen size, language settings, IP address, and crash or performance diagnostic logs.
* **Advertising and Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android), Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice), App Set ID / Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), Unity installation IDs, and push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service).
* **Gameplay and Usage Data:** Session duration, levels completed, achievements unlocked, in-game events, and interactions with advertisements or optional push notifications (in *Kelsi Davies: Haunt Escape*).
* **Approximate Location:** Coarse geographic location (such as country or city) inferred from your IP address by analytics and advertising providers.
* **Platform Account Identifiers:** If you sign in with **Google Play Games Services** (Android) or **Apple Game Center** (iOS), the platform accesses your player ID, public nickname/gamer tag, and achievement progress.
* **Purchase Records:** If you make In-App Purchases (such as unlocking episodes, content, or removing ads), transactions are processed exclusively by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive confirmation tokens and purchase status to unlock your content, but we never receive or store your credit card or billing details.
* **Support Communications (User Content):** If you email us or reach out for support, we collect your email address and any information or screenshots you choose to send us.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information collected through our Games is used to:
* Operate core game features, save progress, sync achievements, and unlock In-App Purchases.
* Serve, mediate, and measure advertisements (including personalized or contextual ads, depending on your region and device permissions).
* Analyze gameplay performance, player progression, and crash diagnostics to fix bugs and improve game balance.
* Deliver optional push notifications (when enabled by you in *Kelsi Davies: Haunt Escape*).
* Prevent fraud and comply with applicable regional age-assurance laws using the **Google Play Age Signals API** (Android) and **Apple Declared Age Range API** (iOS). Age signals are used strictly to deliver age-appropriate experiences and are never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers
Not every SDK is active in every Game or platform (for example, *Taya's Alphabet* does not use push notifications). Across our Games, we use the following third-party providers:

* **Unity Technologies** (Unity Engine, Unity Analytics, Unity LevelPlay / ironSource mediation, Unity Ads, and Unity Push Notifications):  
  https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy  
  https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
* **Google** (Google Play Services, Google Play Games, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Firebase Cloud Messaging):  
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
* **Apple** (Apple App Store, Apple Game Center, StoreKit In-App Purchases, and Apple Push Notification service):  
  https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If you tap an external link inside a Game (such as a community Discord link), you will leave the Game and be subject to that third party's privacy policy.

## 5. Data Security and Retention
All data transmitted by our third-party SDKs is encrypted in transit using standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS). Analytics, crash logs, and advertising mediation data collected by Unity and ironSource are retained in accordance with their default retention schedules (typically 6 to 13 months) before being anonymized or deleted.

## 6. Your Privacy Choices
You can manage or limit data collection at any time:
* **Opt Out of Personalized Ads / Tracking:**
  * **Android:** Open **Settings > Google > Ads** to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  * **iOS:** Open **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking** to revoke tracking permissions, or reset your advertising identifier.
* **Push Notifications:** Disable notifications anytime in your Android or iOS device settings.
* **Platform Gaming Accounts:** Manage or revoke access to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center through your Google or Apple account settings.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Our Games are intended for general and older audiences (**Kelsi Davies: Haunt Escape** is rated Teen / 13+, and **Taya's Alphabet** is rated Everyone 10+ / 9+) and are **not directed to children under the age of 13** (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new game features, SDK updates, or legal requirements. Changes take effect when posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
